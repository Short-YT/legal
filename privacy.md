# Ciencia Imposible Shorts — Privacy Policy

Ciencia Imposible Shorts is a personal tool used only by its owner. It accesses the owner's TikTok account solely to read basic profile information (user.info.basic) and to upload videos as drafts (video.upload). Access tokens are stored locally on the owner's computer and are never shared. No data from other TikTok users is collected, stored, or shared.
